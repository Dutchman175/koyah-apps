Capsule Fill Trainer

Practice on a JTJ-V style fill station. Empty capsule bottoms sit in a spinning stainless disc. The auger drops powder into each capsule as it passes under the hopper. Auger at 50 is the normal setting. Raise turntable speed to give each capsule less time under the auger and lower the fill.

  • Higher auger = more powder. Higher turntable = less fill (less dwell time).
